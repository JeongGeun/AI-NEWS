English Summary: This paper addresses the grounding gap in autonomous web agents powered by LLMs, where textual workflow skills lack executability and code-based skills lack interpretability for error recovery. English Summary: This research investigates the delayed generalization phenomenon (grokking) in transformer models trained on arithmetic tasks, where long gaps occur between training-set fitting and sudden generalization. The authors argue this delay stems from limited access to already-learned structure rather than failure to acquire the structure initially. They examine encoder-decoder arithmetic models and one-step Collatz prediction to support their findings.

핵심 키워드: arXiv, transformers, encoder-decoder models, Collatz prediction
