English Summary: This practical guide demonstrates deploying MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers on Cloudflare Workers for free, enabling team-wide access rather than local-only usage. Leveraging Cloudflare’s free tier with 100,000 daily requests and Durable Objects for state management, the approach achieves near-zero cold starts and simplifies secret management.

핵심 키워드: Cloudflare Workers, MCP (Model Context Protocol), Durable Objects, wrangler, Claude Desktop, SQLite
