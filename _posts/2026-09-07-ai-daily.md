**English Summary**: As AI-powered incident response tools become increasingly capable at resolving routine system failures, engineers are losing hands-on practice opportunities to develop intuition about their systems. The paradox of automation means that while AI handles routine incidents, engineers lack the practical experience needed to respond effectively when novel, high-severity incidents occur that automation cannot solve.

**핵심 키워드**: LinkedIn, Lisanne Bainbridge, AI SREs, Ironies of Automation
