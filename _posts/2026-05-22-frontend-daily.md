**English Summary**: This Smashing Magazine article introduces sibling-index() and sibling-count() CSS functions that enable staggered animation cascades in a single CSS line without :nth-child() rules or JavaScript. It eliminates the need for hardcoded selectors or Sass loops, providing a scalable solution that works whether you have 5 or 5,000 items.
