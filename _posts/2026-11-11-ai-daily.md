**English Summary**: The most critical challenge in conversational voice AI is not LLM response latency but interruption (barge-in) latency. The article dissects why standard voice pipelines lag (typically 540ms-1,170ms total) and presents HardCall, an open-source solution achieving 52ms interruption latency through architectural optimization.
